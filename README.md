# Superstore-Sales-Analystics-Dashboard

1. Project Overview

The Superstore Business Intelligence & Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing retail sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional sales trends. The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

This project helps management understand business performance, identify profitable products and regions, evaluate customer segments, and discover opportunities to improve sales and profitability.

2. Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales, profit, and order performance.
Identify top-performing and low-performing products.
Evaluate sales and profitability across regions and states.
Analyze monthly sales trends and business growth.
Understand customer segment performance.
Identify products and regions requiring improvement.
Develop actionable, data-driven business recommendations.

3. Tools & Technologies
Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn for data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory data analysis.
SQL: Business queries, aggregations, ranking, and sales analysis.
Power BI: Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, DAX measures, charts, and slicers.
GitHub: Project documentation, version control, and portfolio presentation.

4. Dataset Description

The Superstore dataset contains retail transaction data used to analyze sales performance, customer activity, product categories, shipping methods, and geographical trends.

Key fields may include:

Order ID
Order Date
Ship Date
Customer ID
Customer Name
Segment
Region
State
Category
Sub-Category
Product Name
Sales

Note: Profit, Discount, and other fields will be analyzed if they are available in the dataset.

5. Project Workflow 
Step 1: Data Cleaning Using Python
Inspect dataset structure and data types.
Identify and handle missing values.
Check duplicate records.
Standardize column names.
Convert date columns into appropriate formats.
Perform exploratory data analysis.

Step 2: Business Analysis Using SQL
Calculate total sales and order counts.
Analyze region-wise and state-wise sales.
Identify top-performing categories and products.
Rank customers by sales contribution.
Compare product and regional performance.
Step 3: Interactive Dashboard Using Power BI
Create KPI cards for key business metrics.
Analyze monthly sales trends.
Compare regional and category performance.
Identify top customers and products.
Add interactive slicers and filters.
Highlight business opportunities through visual analysis.

6. Key Business Questions
What are the total sales and total number of orders?
How many unique customers placed orders?
How do sales change month by month?
Which region generates the highest sales?
Which states contribute the most to sales?
Which product categories perform best?
Which sub-categories generate the highest sales?
Which customer segment contributes the most revenue?
Who are the top 10 customers by sales?
Which products have the lowest sales?
Which regions require greater business attention?
What actions could help improve overall sales performance?

7. Dashboard Features
Executive KPI cards
Monthly sales trend analysis
Region-wise sales comparison
State-wise geographical analysis
Category and sub-category performance
Top 10 customer analysis
Product performance analysis
Interactive date, region, category, and segment slicers

The final dashboard features depend on the available dataset columns and completed Power BI implementation.

Conclusion

The Superstore Business Intelligence & Sales Analysis project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

Python supports data preparation and exploratory analysis, SQL answers important business questions, and Power BI presents the results through interactive visualizations.

The project showcases practical skills in data cleaning, querying, KPI development, dashboard creation, and business-focused analysis. It provides a portfolio example of how raw retail data can be transformed into useful information for management decision-making.
